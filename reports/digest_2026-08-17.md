### Evaluation Frameworks & Benchmarks

### [Benchmarking the Benchmarks:Evaluating Benchmarks for Conversational Agents](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.06329v1)
*Source: tavily | 2026-08-17*

The paper introduces complexity and coverage metrics for conversational-agent benchmarks, emphasizing the need to include tasks where users request actions that conflict with domain policies. It argues that high-quality benchmarks must explicitly measure whether agents correctly refuse or redirect such requests instead of hallucinating compliance. This is immediately usable for hardening our Test Framework’s outcome checks and persona objectives around policy enforcement.
