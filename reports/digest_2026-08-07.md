# Agent Eval Digest — 2026-08-07

## TL;DR
A large-scale review found that synthetic users systematically fail to match human preferences and introduce self-favoring bias in LLM evaluators. This directly challenges the core assumption behind Connectly’s Test Framework, where simulated personas drive multi-turn regression testing. Teams relying on synthetic participants for edge-case discovery or outcome evaluation should expect high false-negative rates on real failure modes.

Contamination from training data further undermines any attempt to replicate published human studies with LLMs. For Connectly this means our current persona objectives may be systematically under-detecting real user friction and that any auto-eval layer on top of Test Framework conversations is likely to reinforce the same bias.
