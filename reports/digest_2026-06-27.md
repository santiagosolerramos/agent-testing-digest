### Evaluation Frameworks & Benchmarks

### [Building Evals That Don't Lie: How to Make AI Evaluation Reliable in Production](https://dev.to/abdul___rehman/building-evals-that-dont-lie-how-to-make-ai-evaluation-reliable-in-production-44nn)
*Source: rss | 2026-06-27*

The author ran an LLM scoring pipeline over a large batch of job listings and obtained well-distributed scores with confidence values, only to discover on manual review that most high-confidence scores were attached to irrelevant matches. The core insight is that surface-level numeric stability in LLM judges does not equal correctness, and that production evals require additional verification layers beyond the initial model call.

This maps directly to the risk in our Test Framework when personas run multi-turn conversations and an LLM judge decides whether tool calls or handoffs succeeded.
