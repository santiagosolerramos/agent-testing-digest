**Acción concreta:** Prototype wrapping our existing Test Framework personas inside lm-eval-harness task definitions so every nightly run produces a single scalar “Test Framework score” instead of scattered pass/fail logs.

### 3. [Evaluating AI-Simulated Behavior: Insights from Three Studies on ...](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ai-simulations-studies)
