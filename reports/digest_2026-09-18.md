### Synthetic User & Persona Simulation
### [When Synthetic Users Fail: A Cross-Domain Benchmark of LLM-Simulated Human Survey Responses](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2607.26348)
*Source: tavily | 2026-09-18*

The paper introduces a single protocol run across four models and multiple domains to measure when LLM-simulated respondents diverge from real human behavior. It surfaces systematic failure modes (over-refusal, hallucinated demographics, inconsistent preferences) that are directly relevant to building reliable test personas. The framework can be reused to validate new personas before they enter the regression suite.
