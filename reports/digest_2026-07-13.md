### [Synthetic Data vs Synthetic Users: What's the Difference](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/teresatorres_lets-talk-about-the-difference-between-synthetic-activity-7406490644023742466-sIfd)

Teresa Torres highlights that synthetic data (varied LLM inputs) and synthetic users (behavioral personas with goals and traits) solve different problems when testing agent outputs. The distinction matters for teams building goal-oriented conversational agents that must handle diverse inputs without losing objective tracking.
