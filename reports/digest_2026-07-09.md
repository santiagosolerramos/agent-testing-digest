Sierra released τ³-Bench, expanding agent evaluation into knowledge retrieval and voice—directly relevant for Connectly’s multi-turn WhatsApp agents. ### Synthetic User & Persona Simulation

### [Your Synthetic Users primer - by Caitlin Sullivan](https://aicustomerresearch.substack.com/p/your-synthetic-users-primer)
*Source: tavily | 2026-07-09*

Recent studies (Twin-25-500, Park, Toubia) show that once sufficient real data exists, the specific synthetic-user generation method matters less than data depth.
