### Synthetic User & Persona Simulation

### [Synthetic Users vs Real Customers for AI Research: When Each Wins](https://www.userintuition.ai/reference-guides/synthetic-users-vs-real-customers-for-ai-research)
*Source: tavily | 2026-08-06*

The piece argues that LLMs are most useful early, when you need to surface common behavioral patterns and hypotheses before investing in real interviews. It explicitly recommends running the model as a customer persona to generate discussion-guide ideas that would otherwise be missed. For Connectly this means we can systematically pre-screen edge cases (order cancellation with partial refunds, language switching mid-thread, ambiguous product queries) in our Test Framework before any human touches the test.
