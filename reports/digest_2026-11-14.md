### Synthetic User & Persona Simulation

### Synthetic Users for Early-Stage Validation | https://qualz.ai/blog/synthetic-users-early-validation
*Source: tavily | 2026-11-14*

The post outlines a repeatable workflow: create rich persona profiles (background, goals, tech comfort, domain experience), calibrate them by comparing synthetic outputs to real-user data, then use the calibrated personas for research or testing. The calibration step is the most interesting part because it turns ad-hoc persona writing into something measurable and auditable. For Connectly this is immediately useful for reducing false positives in multi-turn WhatsApp tests and for catching subtle regressions when we change prompts or tools.
