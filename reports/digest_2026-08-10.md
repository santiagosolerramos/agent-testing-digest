## Top Findings

### Synthetic User & Persona Simulation

### [Automated end-to-end testing for conversational agents](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0164121225003541)
*Source: tavily | 2026-08-10*

The work presents a two-part system: a highly configurable user simulator that can vary goals, utterance length, spelling errors, and other behavioral traits, plus a DSL for authoring deterministic test scenarios. The simulator runs full multi-turn conversations against the agent under test and feeds outcomes into automated checks—exactly the pattern our Test Framework already uses with mock data. This is the first public description of a simulator explicitly designed to surface edge-case communication styles rather than happy-path dialogues, which speaks directly to our open question on writing personas that find real failures.
