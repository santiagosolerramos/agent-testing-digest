# Agent Eval Digest — 2026-05-18

## TL;DR
Conversational AI testing practices and WhatsApp-specific UX gaps dominated today's relevant items. The most actionable signals are around probabilistic multi-turn validation and handling voice notes—both directly stress-test the assumptions in our persona-driven Test Framework. RAG-focused metrics remain secondary for our goal-oriented agents.

## 🎯 Top 3 para Connectly

### 1. [Conversational AI Testing: End-to-End Validation for Dialogue Systems](https://www.coval.ai/blog/conversational-ai-testing)
**Por qué importa para Connectly:** Directly tackles the non-deterministic, multi-turn nature of our Test Framework personas and the need to validate full dialogues rather than single turns.  
**Acción concreta:** Extend our persona YAMLs with explicit variation sets (paraphrases, interruptions, accents) and add an end-to-end outcome checker modeled on the article’s validation surface.

### 2. [Using WhatsApp for AI Customer Support: A Guide | Open](https://www.open.cx/blog/whatsapp-ai-customer-support-guide)
**Por qué importa para Connectly:** Highlights the voice-note blind spot that most WhatsApp agent platforms still ignore—exactly the kind of real-world failure mode our personas should surface.  
**Acción concreta:** Add a “voice-note user” persona trait to the Test Framework and define expected behavior (transcription + fallback) as a regression test.

### 3. [RAG Evaluation with RAGAS: Measuring Faithfulness, Context Precision, and Recall in Production](https://dev.to/anna_danilec/rag-evaluation-with-ragas-measuring-faithfulness-context-precision-and-recall-in-production-31hj)
**Por qué importa para Connectly:** Offers a lightweight, no-labels metric suite we could run in CI whenever retrieval components inside our agents change.  
**Acción concreta:** Pilot RAGAS faithfulness + context-recall on a subset of order-resolution test cases that rely on product catalog retrieval.

### Multi-Turn & Dialogue Evaluation
### [Conversational AI Testing: End-to-End Validation for Dialogue Systems](https://www.coval.ai/blog/conversational-ai-testing)
*Source: tavily | 2026-05-18*

The post argues that conversational systems require validation across paraphrases, voice artifacts, interruptions, and latency—far beyond deterministic unit tests. This maps almost 1:1 to the multi-turn persona conversations we already run and gives concrete ideas for expanding our evaluation surface.

### Evaluation Frameworks & Benchmarks
### [RAG Evaluation with RAGAS: Measuring Faithfulness, Context Precision, and Recall in Production](https://dev.to/anna_danilec/rag-evaluation-with-ragas-measuring-faithfulness-context-precision-and-recall-in-production-31hj)
*Source: rss | 2026-05-18*

RAGAS provides reference-free metrics that separate retrieval failures from generation hallucinations and can be added to CI pipelines. While our agents are primarily goal-oriented rather than open RAG, the same faithfulness checks could flag hallucinated order details or policy answers. Worth a targeted pilot on catalog-dependent flows.
