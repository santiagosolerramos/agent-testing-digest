# Agent Eval Digest — 2026-09-24

## TL;DR
Two WhatsApp-focused items today highlight where production AI agents diverge from scripted chatbots and deliver measurable resolution rates (up to 65%) at 90% lower cost using newer OpenAI models. Both directly inform how Connectly should benchmark its Test Framework personas against real multilingual, multi-channel deployments.

## 🎯 Top 3 para Connectly

### 1. [WhatsApp AI Agents: Where They Add Value Beyond Chatbots](https://emovur.com/blog/whatsapp-business-api/whatsapp-ai-agents-vs-chatbots)
**Por qué importa para Connectly:** Directly addresses the exact boundary our Test Framework must test — when a persona should trigger tool calls or handoff instead of following fixed paths.
**Acción concreta:** Add a new persona trait in the Test Framework YAML that forces the agent to decide between scripted reply vs. dynamic action on WhatsApp.

### 2. [Ringg’s AI agents resolve up to 65% of customer calls with OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/ringg)
**Por qué importa para Connectly:** Shows a live competitor achieving 65% autonomous resolution on WhatsApp + voice with GPT-5.6 at 10× lower cost; gives concrete metrics to beat in our regression suite.
**Acción concreta:** Run a side-by-side eval of our current personas against the resolution-rate and language-coverage claims using the same mock catalog data.

### 3. [Launch HN: Skillsync (YC W26) – AI chat sessions made portable across agents](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49743049)
**Por qué importa para Connectly:** Portable session format could let us export failing multi-turn Test Framework conversations and replay them on competitor agents for regression comparison.
**Acción concreta:** Prototype exporting a subset of our persona dialogues into the Skillsync format and test replay on a Decagon or Sierra sandbox.
