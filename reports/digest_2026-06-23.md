### Regression Testing & CI/CD for Agents
### [Prevent AI Agent Failures with Reliable Testing Frameworks 2025](https://onereach.ai/blog/why-testing-is-critical-for-ai-agents)
*Source: tavily | 2026-06-23*

The post argues that UI-focused regression tools cannot verify the underlying decision logic or tool-use correctness of conversational agents and calls for dedicated behavior-level test suites. It highlights the risk of silent regressions after prompt or model changes — a pain point we have discussed in our own CI pipeline. The piece is light on implementation details but validates the direction of our persona-based regression suite.
