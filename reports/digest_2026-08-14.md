### Synthetic User & Persona Simulation

### We Ran an Experiment with Synthetic Users: Here’s What We Found | Userbrain Blog
*Source: tavily | 2026-08-14*

The post reports that LLM-based synthetic users completed tasks via exhaustive, logical enumeration rather than the erratic paths real users take. The same pattern was observed in the 2025 UXAgent CHI paper on e-commerce usability testing. For Connectly this means our current persona objectives may be too goal-rational, missing the ambiguity and backtracking that surface real edge cases in WhatsApp conversations.
