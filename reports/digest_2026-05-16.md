### Evaluation Frameworks & Benchmarks

### [AI Agent Evaluation in Production (2026 Guide)](https://thinking.inc/en/blue-ocean/agentic/ai-agent-evaluation-production/)
*Source: tavily | 2026-05-16*

The guide distills production benchmark design into four principles: representative sampling of real user inputs, inclusion of edge/adversarial/multilingual cases, continuous versioning, and human expert validation of references. This maps almost 1:1 to the gaps in our current Test Framework, which still leans heavily on curated happy-path personas. Implementing these principles would immediately improve signal-to-noise on our multi-turn WhatsApp evaluations.
