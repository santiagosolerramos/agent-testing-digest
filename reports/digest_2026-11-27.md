### Synthetic User & Persona Simulation

### [Synthetic User Testing — Definition, Example & How It's Used](https://www.qabash.com/glossary/ai-testing/synthetic-user-testing)
*Source: tavily | 2026-11-27*

Synthetic user testing uses LLM-driven personas to generate realistic usage paths and edge cases at scale before real-user testing. The approach emphasizes volume and speed that human teams cannot match while still producing deterministic mock-data interactions. This is a near-exact description of Connectly's current Test Framework and gives us language to tighten persona objectives and behavioral traits.
