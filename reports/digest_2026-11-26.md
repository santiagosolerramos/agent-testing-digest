Sierra dropped 𝜏³-bench (knowledge track) and a practical testing guide the same day, giving the clearest public signal yet on how a top-tier customer agent company structures regression suites and edge-case coverage. ## 🎯 Top 3 para Connectly

### 1. [𝜏³-bench | Sierra](https://sierra.ai/resources/research/tau-3-bench-knowledge)
**Por qué importa para Connectly:** Sierra’s new knowledge benchmark directly targets the same multi-turn, tool-using customer agents we run on WhatsApp, and they are a direct competitor.  
**Acción concreta:** Download the benchmark and map our existing Test Framework personas against its knowledge categories to see coverage gaps.

### 2. [How to test and benchmark AI agents | Sierra](https://sierra.ai/learn/how-to-test-ai-agents)
**Por qué importa para Connectly:** Explicitly recommends building regression suites from post-launch failures and covering policy boundaries, escalation, ambiguity, and multi-system disagreements—exactly our open questions.  
**Acción concreta:** Add a “post-incident regression” section to our persona YAML schema and start versioning those test cases.

### 3. [WhatsApp AI Customer Service Upgrade Guide](https://chuhaiwa.app/articles/en/legacy/whatsapp-ai-customer-service-upgrade-guide)
**Por qué importa para Connectly:** The only item focused on WhatsApp agents; lists the exact metrics we should track (task completion, handoff rate, error rate) for production evaluation.  
**Acción concreta:** Align our current evaluation dashboard with the five metrics they recommend and add human-handoff rate as a first-class signal.
