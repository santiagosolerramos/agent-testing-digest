**Acción concreta:** Prototype a nightly CI job that replays 50 high-value personas against the latest agent build and fails the pipeline on regression in tool calls or handoff behavior.

### [AI Agent Evaluation Framework: The Triangle 2026](https://dev.to/akaranjkar08/ai-agent-evaluation-framework-the-triangle-2026-17cm)
*Source: rss | 2026-07-02*

The WOWHOW Triangle scores agents on Tool-Selection Accuracy, Planning Quality, and Rollback-ability using a weighted harmonic mean. ### Regression Testing & CI/CD for Agents
### [Evaluation Strategies for Generative Agents in Conversational Agents/Dialogflow CX](https://discuss.google.dev/t/evaluation-strategies-for-generative-agents-in-conversational-agents-dialogflow-cx/335387)
*Source: tavily | 2026-07-02*

Google engineers describe triggering scripted multi-turn conversations against the agent endpoint after every deployment, storing responses, and running automated rubric scoring in CI. They note that combining automated rubrics with periodic human review is the pragmatic pattern that scales. This mirrors the exact workflow our Test Framework already supports and validates that API-driven persona replay is the right direction for continuous regression.
