## Top Findings

### Synthetic User & Persona Simulation

### [Synthetic Users in AI Concept Testing: Why the Tautology Breaks](https://www.userintuition.ai/reference-guides/synthetic-users-in-ai-concept-testing)
*Source: tavily | 2026-09-05*

The piece argues that the case for synthetic users in AI testing is tautological: we trust LLMs to simulate users because the product itself is an LLM, yet this creates an unexamined feedback loop and ignores recruitment-cost realities. For Connectly this matters because our Test Framework already generates fully synthetic multi-turn WhatsApp conversations; if the personas are only good at language-based reasoning tasks, we may be systematically missing non-linguistic failure modes (tone, cultural nuance, ambiguous intent).
