## Top Findings

### Synthetic User & Persona Simulation

### [When Synthetic Users Fail: A Cross-Domain Benchmark of LLM-Simulated Human Survey Responses](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.26348v1)
*Source: tavily | 2026-09-13*

Across two domains and multiple models, LLM synthetic users fail to beat non-LLM baselines at the individual response level and systematically over-predict attitudes from demographics alone. This is a direct warning for Test Framework: our personas may be producing overly deterministic behavior that masks real edge cases. The paper's protocol for measuring "over-determination" can be repurposed as an automated check in our persona YAML.
