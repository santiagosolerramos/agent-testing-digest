### [7 Top Synthetic User Testing Platforms to Watch in 2026](https://www.uxia.app/blog/7-top-synthetic-user-testing-platforms-to-watch-in-2026)
*Source: tavily | 2026-10-17*

Simulate Users lets teams run LLM-powered market surveys by generating responses from hundreds of synthetic users to test product ideas, messaging, and segmentation at low cost. The approach emphasizes speed and scale over traditional panels, producing deterministic synthetic interactions that can be replayed. For Connectly this suggests a practical way to auto-generate diverse personas that stress-test goal-oriented conversations (e.g., order recovery or FAQ flows) instead of relying solely on hand-written test cases.
