# Agent Eval Digest — 2026-07-24

## TL;DR
Today’s strongest signals are new agent evaluation frameworks that shift focus from output accuracy to behavioral correctness, concrete guidance on hardening tool-use validation, and practical synthetic data techniques for building regression datasets. These directly address Connectly’s open questions on scaling evals, catching tool-call regressions, and writing personas that surface real edge cases rather than happy paths.
