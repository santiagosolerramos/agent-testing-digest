### Synthetic User & Persona Simulation

### [We Tried Synthetic User Testing So You Don't Have To](https://growth-designers.beehiiv.com/p/we-tried-synthetic-user-testing-so-you-don-t-have-to)
*Source: tavily | 2026-11-15*

The post reviews three early tools that use multi-agent LLM setups or behavior-modeling to create synthetic users for prototype and flow testing. Synthetic Users partners with Comcast NBCUniversal, Uxia focuses on usability/accessibility issues in Barcelona, and Blok generates “virtual users modeled off actual product behavior” specifically to stress-test onboarding variants. For Connectly this is immediately actionable because our Test Framework already runs full multi-turn conversations against personas; adopting or benchmarking against these generators could reduce the manual effort of writing traits that reliably trigger tool calls, handoffs, or language-specific edge cases.
